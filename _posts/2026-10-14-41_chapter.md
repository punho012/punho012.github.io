## Introduction

Operating systems are crucial element of OPSEC. They are the engine of computers. It is essentila to keep operating systems up-to-date for security and use opem-source operating system for privacy and anonimity.

The main desktop operating systems are:
- Windows
- MAC
- Linux

**Windows**
From a privacy point of view windows is the worst in the list. They collect a huge amount of analytics, enforce online accounts, invasive AI usage.

**MAC**
Mac is a bit better then windows but still force online accounts and strong data collection habit.

**Linux**
From a privacy standpoint linux is the recommended option. It is open-source and secure with many different distros. Users can choose best fit for theor needs. Some of the fits for beginners because it is customized in advanc eand easy to use. Others offer additional privacy features.
