## Phile 32 - Trust

Trust is a fundamental concept in OPSEC. In the digital life when people ue a service they make trust decisions. They must trust in applications, organizations, service providers, VPN providers, operating systems, governments. In general more trust means more convinience, but also more vulnarability. People trust in applications to handle their sensitive data properly. They trust in governments defending them from dangers. They trust in organizations not handing over personal information to governments and private comapanies. The more trust we bring in our lives we are more vulnarable. In the field of OPSEC our goal is limiting the outsourced trust. It is a crucial skill in OPSEC determining the trust boundary, how much trust we can approve. For example if yu use a messaging app you have to trust in the code, the update mechanism, the infrastructure of the service.
