## Introduction
Smartphones plays an important role in modern surveillance. Most people, carry these small computers everywhere, allowing them to collect information,movements, communications, content consumption and interests through the apps they install and the websites they visit. In many cases they also process health-related information such as daily steps and sleeping habits.

On the other hand mobile phones offer various security features, including robust permisison system, application sandboxing and file-based encryption.

There are two major phone operating systems: Android and IOS.

### Android

Android  is an open-source mobile operating system developed by Google.  Android is famous for sending geolocation and other sensitive user data to Google. 
Additionally, many users do not pay enough attention to app permissions when installing applications. Most of these applications request access to sensitive information, including files, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, location, camera and microphone. These permissions can expose a significant amount of personal data, especially when users grant access without considering whether it is necessary.

Among Android devices, Google Pixel phones are particularly worth considering because of their strong hardware security features and support for custom operating systems such as GrapheneOS. More on that later.

### IOS

iOS is the mobile operating system developed by Apple for its iPhone devices.
Regarding privacy, Apple also collects user data through its services and built-in features. However, its business model differs heavily from Google, as it relies less on advertising, especially targeted advertising. Apple also operates its own advertising busniess but the collected information primarily remains in-house for product improvement purposes.

However, users can improve privacy by disabling certain telemetry features, and hardening the privacy and security settings built into the operating systems.

## Graphene OS

GrapheneOS is the strongest option when it comes to mobile privacy and security.

GrapheneOS provides additional security hardening and privacy improvements. It has a hardened memory allocator, network and sensor permissions, and various other security features. GrapheneOS also provides firmware updates and signed builds, supporting Android Verified Boot.

Google Pixel phones are curently the only devices that meet GrapheneOS's hardware security requirements.

## Final Takeawy

Smartphones are among the most personal devices we use, making their security and privacy particularly important. Both Android and iOS offer built-in security features, but their privacy characteristics and data collection practices differ. For users who want greater control over their mobile environment, GrapheneOS on a supported Pixel device is a strong option. However, the operating system alone cannot guarantee privacy — app permissions, configuration, and everyday usage habits remain equally important.
